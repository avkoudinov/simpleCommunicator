This Privacy Policy determines the procedure for processing and protecting the personal data of users of this Site.

1. Terms and Definitions

  • Personal data — any information relating to a directly or indirectly identified or identifiable natural person (user).
  • Processing of personal data — any actions (operations) with personal data, including collection, recording, systematization, accumulation, storage, clarification, use, transfer, deletion, and destruction.
  • User — a natural person registered on the Forum and/or using its functionality.
  • Forum — this Site, providing users with the opportunity to communicate, exchange information, and participate in discussions.

2. Purposes of Personal Data Processing

The Forum processes users' personal data for the following purposes:

  • registration and authentication of users on the site;
  • ensuring the functioning and security of the Forum;
  • providing access to the services and features of the Forum;
  • content moderation and maintaining order on the platform;
  • communication with users (including via email and through notifications on the site);
  • analysis of user activity to improve the operation of the Forum;
  • fulfillment of legal requirements.

3. Types of Processed Personal Data

When registering and using the Forum, the user may provide the following data:

  • username (nickname);
  • email address;
  • password (stored in encrypted form);
  • IP address and browser/device data (automatically collected by the server);
  • date and time of registration, history of activity on the site;
  • additional information posted by the user in the profile and messages (avatar, signature, personal data in public messages).

4. Legal Grounds for Processing

The processing of personal data is carried out on the following grounds:

  • the user's consent given during registration and/or use of the Forum's functionality;
  • the necessity of executing an agreement (user agreement) between the user and the Forum owner;
  • requirements of applicable legislation regarding personal data and information.

5. Procedure and Conditions of Processing

  • Data collection occurs during registration, profile filling, and posting messages on the Forum.
  • Data is stored on secure servers.
  • Authorized employees of the Forum administration have access to personal data.
  • Personal data is not transferred to third parties, except in cases of:
    • user consent;
    • requirements of authorities in accordance with the law;
    • the need to protect the rights and legitimate interests of the Forum or other users.

6. User Rights

The user has the right to:

  • receive information about the existence of their personal data and review it;
  • request clarification, blocking, or deletion of their personal data;
  • withdraw consent to the processing of personal data (via profile settings or by sending a request to the administration);
  • appeal the actions of the Forum administration to the authorized body for the protection of the rights of personal data subjects or in court.

To exercise these rights, the user may contact the Forum administration in simple written form via the contact form.

7. Data Protection Measures

The Forum takes the following measures to protect personal data:

  • use of encryption (HTTPS) during data transfer;
  • storage of passwords in encrypted (hashed) form;
  • restricting access to databases;
  • regular data backup;
  • monitoring and prevention of unauthorized access.

8. Cookies and Web Analytics

The Forum uses cookies to:

  • save user sessions;
  • personalize the interface;
  • collect traffic statistics (using analytics services such as Yandex Metrica or Google Analytics).

The user can disable cookies in their browser settings; however, this may limit the Forum's functionality.

9. Changes to the Privacy Policy

The Forum administration has the right to make changes to this policy. The updated version is published on the site indicating the date of the last revision. Users are encouraged to periodically check the text of the policy.

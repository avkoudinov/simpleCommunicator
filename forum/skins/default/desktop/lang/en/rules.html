Forum Rules

Revised as of: 2017-12-29

  1. No one is forcing you to join this forum, and if you don't like something here, no one is forcing you to stay.
  2. Judge and be judged, and be the first to cast a stone!
  3. Others tolerate you, so you must tolerate them!
  4. Use your brain, Luke! Act reasonably and adequately, and everything will be fine. If you have been banned or moderated on this forum, it means you didn't use your brain.
  5. Turn on your brain when posting messages. Once published, they become the property of the forum, and requests to delete them may be rejected. However, personal information or links to personal information will be deleted upon the author's request. Requests should be sent via the contact form, specifying contact details and a link to the message containing personal data, or from a registered account by filing a report on a specific message. Requests from guest nicknames or requests without specific links to the message may be rejected!
  6. The forum was created for communication among visitors from the countries of the former USSR. Since the majority of visitors are from the Russian Federation, this affects the freedoms provided in communication and self-expression to avoid blocking by Roskomnadzor in connection with the laws regarding the Internet adopted in the Russian Federation. If a message, topic, image, or avatar, in the administration's opinion, poses a threat to the forum, it may be deleted for preventive purposes.
  7. It is possible that intelligence agencies of certain countries may obtain access to forum data and logs through the provider. Therefore, when writing messages, proceed from the assumption that intelligence agencies could theoretically find out your IP address.

Who can see IP addresses?

  • The topic moderator does not see IP addresses.
  • The forum moderator sees IP addresses only in the forum they are responsible for.
  • The administrator sees IP addresses in all forums.

Content delivery to search engines

Content of most forums is not delivered to search bots for indexing due to the specifics of the content. Search bots are identified by officially named user-agent signatures, so there is no 100% guarantee that the content is not indexed by any system at all. However, testing in search engines like Yandex and Google shows that they do not index forums protected from indexing.

The appearance of search bots in the "Users Online" line does not mean they are reading this specific forum. This line shows visitors of the resource as a whole. The forum is indexed by search engines only if the search bots are displayed in the "Reading topic" or "Reading forum" lines.

How private are "Private Topics"?

Private messages are implemented as private topics in a special forum. Participants of a private topic can communicate just like in a regular forum topic and can leave the private topic, including the author of the topic themselves.

There are two types of private topics:

  • Private topics for one-on-one communication where no one can add or kick out participants and everyone can leave the topic. They are created by sending a private message to another participant.
  • Private topics with their own moderation. In such topics, the author can add and remove participants, delete and edit messages. Invited participants can leave such a topic, while the author cannot. However, the author can delete it (and restore it if necessary). When deleted, access to the topic for invited participants disappears. Such topics are created by starting a topic in the virtual forum "Private Messages".

If a participant does not wish to receive private messages, they can disable the corresponding feature in the profile settings. In this case, they can continue to communicate in already created private topics, as well as write private messages to other participants themselves.

If a participant does not wish to receive messages in existing private topics, they can leave these topics or put them on ignore.

If someone is harassing you in private topics, sending you private messages, or insistently inviting you to a topic even after you have left it, please report it to the administration, preferably with confirmation via screenshots. Administrators do not have access to read private messages, but they have the ability to block a user from writing private messages.

  1. On other forums, there are interfaces for reading private messages by the administrator; such an interface is absent on this forum. However, there is naturally a technical possibility to read private messages from the database using SQL queries.
  2. The administrator does not read private topics in which they do not participate and does not fulfill requests from other participants to provide the content of such topics. If someone does not believe this, well, it is impossible to prove otherwise. So, when using private messages, you can proceed from the assumption that the administrator could theoretically read them.
  3. Access to a user's account, forum profile, and private messages can be obtained by an attacker using vulnerabilities in the forum web application, web server, or operating system, by infecting the client device with malware, or by the user losing their account access credentials or the device itself.
  4. When communicating with participants, assume that their private conversations with you can be read by their relatives or other persons who have direct access to the client device.
  5. A participant can independently publish private correspondence or transfer access credentials to the account to third parties. There have been such precedents and there may be more.
  6. In the event of a potential transfer of the forum to a successor, and accordingly, when using private messages under a new administrator, you can decide for yourself whether you trust them or not.
  7. If necessary and in accordance with the legislation of the Russian Federation, law enforcement agencies may obtain access to the personal data of a participant and their private messages.
